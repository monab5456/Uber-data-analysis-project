# Uber-data-analysis-project
An end to end data analysis project which analyses about **150,000 UBER ride booking** in Delhi,India across **19 dimension** uncovering ride booking trend, revenue, completion performance, and cancellation patterns.

____

## Project Overview
 - The project follows a complete data analysis workflow starting from data cleaning and preparation using  python followed by SQL for business analysis and  finally Power BI for creating interactive visualization.
 - AI tools are used to troubleshoot DAX and SQL issues,refinement of dashboard visualization designs and final analysis of the project.
 - uncovers ride success rate, cancellations patterns, revenue and peak demand periods.
 - Published the report in Power Bi Services and also scheduled data loading and refreshment using Power BI Services.

----

## Project Objective 
  - Understand ride success rate, cancellations patterns, customer and driver ratings and unfulfilled ride patterns.
  - Identify revenue patterns and peak demand period.

## Key Insights
  - 62% ride success rate, while 13% of the bookings are unfulfilled even before cancellation enters.
  - Ride cancellation are overwhelmingly high from drivers end account for approx. 18%, indicates supply side fulfillment issues.
  - Consistent peak demand occurs during the evening, particularly around 6-7PM, across the week.
  - Highly demanded pickup areas tend to have lower completion rate mostly driven by driver's cancellation.
  - UPI and Cash are the two prime mode of payment favoured by customers.
  - Auto and Go Mini are the frequently booked vehicle types among all and also dominate to the overall revenue generation.
  - Avg. ratings of both driver and customer end is flat with the change in vehicle type.

## Project Summary
 - Analyzed a 150k row Uber ride booking dataset to identify operational and revenue related challenges. The project involved data cleaning and
EDA using python, SQL and Power Bi for business question formulation, querying , analysis and dashboard designing. The final report derived
insights on revenue trends,booking completion rate(approx. 62%), peak demand period and areas, cancellation behavior of customer & driver and
high demand area with low completion rate.
